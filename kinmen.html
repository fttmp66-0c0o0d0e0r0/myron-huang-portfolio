5-Day Route Itinerary

Recommended day-by-day distances, attractions, and local culinary stops

Day 1 · 15km

SW Coast & Houpu

Arrive at KNH airport, pick up rental bike, ride to Deyue Tower, Zhaishan Tunnel, and Houpu old streets for Cantonese congee.

Day 2 · 40km

Lieyu / Lesser Kinmen

Ride across 5.4km Kinmen Bridge, circle Lieyu coastal loop, visit Cat's Eye Stone, Shatou defense trenches, and eat taro shaved ice.

Day 3 · 30km

NW Coast & Guningtou

Explore Guningtou Battle Museum, bullet-riddled Beishan Old Mansion, Cihu sunset estuary, and taste authentic oyster noodles.

Day 4 · 25km

Mt. Taiwu & NE Coast

Ascend Mt. Taiwu summit, witness Shishan Artillery howitzer drill, visit Shanhou Folk Culture Village, and eat Mashan meat buns.

Day 5 · 10km

Jinhu & Flight Home

Complete the bowknot center at Qionglin Tunnel, take pictures at Taihu 3D crosswalk, return bikes, and take afternoon flight home.
